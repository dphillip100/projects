Top three activities to do at Queens

Catch a concert at UBS Arena

From EDM concerts to hockey games, there's so many different kind of events to explore at the UBS Arena. Click here to check out upcoming events from the official UBS arena website.

Explore the Flushing-Meadow Corona Park

Have a picnic, play in a volleyball tournament, go ice skating, or stop a bug-alien from taking over Earth at the Flushing-Meadow Corona Park.

Experience different cultures among the different areas across Queens

Queens is known for its various culture hotspots scattered across it's large region. Pick a country and head over to the locals for great eats and even better company.
